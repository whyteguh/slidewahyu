02 · STRATEGIC PRIORITIES
PRIORITY ONE

Bet bigger where we already win

Our three highest-margin segments generate 61% of revenue at twice the group margin. In FY25 we move from participating everywhere to dominating where it counts.

  • Concentrate capital. 70% of growth funding into segments where we hold #1 or #2 share.

  • Defend the core. Lift enterprise retention from 68% to 85% by FY26.

  • Exit the tail. Divest non-core legacy lines, releasing $140M of working capital.
